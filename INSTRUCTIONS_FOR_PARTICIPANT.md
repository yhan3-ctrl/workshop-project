# Before the session

During the session you will use an AI agent in a supplied project workspace and
talk with the researcher about how you worked. You can choose GitHub Copilot or
Gemini CLI, subject to your account access. There are no purchases required by
the study. Tell the researcher in advance if you do not have access to either
tool so setup can be arranged.

Please do not upload personal project files or connect additional accounts or
services. The project files used for the activities are supplied examples.
The researcher will explain screen and audio recording, consent, and privacy
before recording starts. You do not need to inspect the activities beforehand.

The researcher will send the workspace link and help with setup during the
session. Account sign-in should be completed before screen recording starts.
